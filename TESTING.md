# Manual in-game checks

Build with `~/.dotnet/dotnet build FarmingCapitalist.csproj -p:EnableModDeploy=false` and copy the generated mod into the game only when you are ready to test.

Run the approach regression fixture with `~/.dotnet/dotnet run --project Tests/WorkApproachChecks/WorkApproachChecks.csproj -p:GamePath="/path/to/Stardew Valley"`. It covers passable grass, fallback recovery without initial snapshot membership, and blocked or occupied approach/landing controls. This fixture does not replace the live game checks below.

1. Load a save with at least 500g. Press `B` and hire a worker with the custom appearance flow. Confirm the worker appears in the farmhouse and the hire cost is charged once.
2. Open the worker menu again. Confirm the worker card shows assignment, current state, location, wage, and completed work. Resize the game window and check the menu remains usable.
3. Assign `Water crops`, `Harvest crops`, and `Tend crops` one at a time. From the farmhouse, confirm the worker visibly walks to an existing warp, arrives directly on a safe walkable tile at or beside an eligible crop, and performs the matching work without a farm staging trip. Put a ready crop at Farm (64,20) with its own tile blocked for walking but a neighboring approach open; confirm `Tend crops` reaches and harvests it without repeating a failed route every second. Make one crop completely unreachable while another is reachable; confirm the worker tries another crop without rapid repeated target selection. For a field with exactly one eligible crop, confirm the worker completes the last crop and visibly walks to a farm exit before arriving home.
4. After the worker arrives home, confirm the sprite is stopped in the neutral south-facing standing frame. Confirm the old persisted work assignment remains selected but the worker stays resting and does not immediately leave again.
5. Confirm harvested produce goes to the shipping bin by default. In Storage, select a worker and assign a chest on the farm, then select another worker and assign a different chest inside a building; confirm each worker deposits to their own chest. Fill or remove a worker's selected chest and confirm remaining produce goes to the shipping bin. Confirm regrowing crops retain their regrowth behavior.
6. Assign `Idle` while the worker is travelling. Confirm the route stops, the worker visibly returns to the farmhouse, and the worker does not continue changing crops.
7. Hire multiple workers and assign them work. Confirm their resting tiles are distinct, clear, and away from every farmhouse door/warp and the tile immediately inside/in front of the exit; the player must be able to enter and leave normally.
8. Make a worker's saved farmhouse tile occupied or blocked, then complete work. Confirm it selects another clear farmhouse tile without overlapping the occupant or overwriting the saved roster tile.
9. End the day and reload the save. Confirm identity, appearance, assignment, per-worker chest destination, and worker count persist, with one wage per worker per day. On the next day/reset, confirm a persisted work assignment can run again after the previous day's resting latch is cleared.
10. Try hiring with insufficient funds, hiring at the worker cap, dismissing one worker, and dismissing all workers. Confirm failed actions do not charge money and other workers remain intact.
11. In multiplayer, confirm the host alone performs hiring, assignment, dismissal, wages, movement, crop changes, and save writes; clients observe the mirrored worker state and each worker's storage choice without changing them.
12. Use `workers help`, `workers status`, and `workers assign <id> <water|harvest|tend|debris|idle>` to inspect and control workers from the SMAPI console.
13. Use the action button on each worker while it is idle, working, and returning home. Confirm a normal portrait dialogue box opens with the worker's display name, the line matches its assignment, and talking again on the same day repeats the same line.
14. Sleep to the next day and talk to the worker again. Confirm its line advances predictably. Test a Content Patcher `EditData` change targeting `Mods/Cleanpup.FarmingCapitalist/WorkerDialogue` and confirm the edited line appears.
15. Hire one Farmer and one Forager. Confirm the upper-right job dropdown in the creation screen saves the chosen profession, and that old saves load existing workers as Farmers.
16. Open Jobs and switch between the Farmer and Forager. Confirm both offer `Clear debris` alongside their existing profession orders; the Forager also sees a forage-area selector.
17. Cycle the Forager's area through Farm, Cindersap Forest, Town, Mountain, Beach, Bus Stop, Backwoods, Railroad, and Secret Woods. Confirm caves, mines, sewers, and interiors never appear, and changing area cancels the old route and returns the worker home until a new order is chosen.
18. Place or find multiple forage items and trees with obstacles around some of them. Confirm the worker chooses a reachable target, approaches an adjacent walkable tile, never crosses fences or water on foot, and never lands inside a blocked tile.
19. Run all three Forager orders. Confirm wild forage is removed and stored; ordinary trees exclude mahogany; hardwood includes mahogany trees, large stumps, and hollow logs; wood, sap, seeds, hardwood, and other vanilla drops go to that worker's selected chest or shipping-bin fallback. Cut several ordinary trees in a row and check that the game stays responsive when each tree falls and its drops are stored.
20. From the farmhouse, send a Forager to Cindersap Forest. Confirm it walks visibly to a reachable existing warp, then arrives directly at a safe approach tile for the Forest job without walking through intermediate maps. After the job, confirm it walks to a reachable existing warp and arrives directly on its reserved farmhouse resting tile. Repeat with that tile occupied and confirm the worker chooses another clear, valid resting tile without overlapping the occupant or freezing the game.
21. On the Farm, block one hardwood approach with weeds, loose stones, or small twigs while leaving another hardwood target reachable. Confirm the worker takes the open route without clearing litter. Then make a target reachable only through one of each small litter type. Confirm it walks to the blocker, clears only what is needed, resumes the job, and stores fiber, stone, or wood drops in the selected chest or shipping bin. Confirm older nearby player drops remain on the ground. For stone or weeds with more than one durability step, confirm logged progress advances through each cooldown and the litter is removed before the calculated safeguard. Recreate a stalled work-route debris approach near Farm (5,45) with another hardwood target available; confirm the worker excludes that failed debris tile, checks another work route, and does not switch to the home route merely because the approach stalled. With no alternate work route, confirm the bounded search eventually returns home without clearing large obstacles.
22. Block every approach to the remaining hardwood with large trees, hollow logs, stumps, or boulders. Confirm the worker leaves those obstacles intact, returns home, and reports the blockage in plain language on the next conversation. Talk again and confirm normal dialogue resumes. Repeat with a save and reload before talking; in multiplayer, speak to the worker as a client and confirm the report is acknowledged once for everyone. Place a chest, fence, or crop across the route and confirm it is never removed or reported as a large natural obstacle.
23. Near 22:00, send a worker toward blocked forager work and confirm it starts the home route when work hours end. Change the assignment while the worker is walking to litter and confirm it stops that job without changing the tile. If small debris blocks the route to all Farm exits, confirm the worker clears only the debris needed and then walks out. If no small-debris route to an authored exit exists, confirm it arrives at a clear reserved farmhouse tile by the last-resort warp. Confirm the warp is never used when a visible exit route or clearable debris route exists, and that a missing-path report appears only if safe farmhouse placement fails.
24. On the hardwood regression save, assign Worker Two to Cut hardwood in Farm. After clumps (4,43) and (8,47), confirm it can select the stump at (14,48) via the grassy approach (13,50) or another reachable approach, without cutting the grass or returning home early. Repeat with every reachable approach covered in grass, including departure from FarmHouse. Temporarily occupy an approach with the farmer or another NPC, then test an actual wall, chest, NPC barrier, or large obstacle: the worker must neither overlap nor clear these blockers. If debris clearing exposes an approach absent from the initial search, confirm fallback logs can report `search-snapshot match=False` with `currently eligible match=True` and then resume the resource job. Recheck Tend crops, drop storage, and the visible return through an existing warp.
25. Assign `Clear debris` to a Farmer. Place one-tile weeds, loose stones, and small fallen branches on the Farm with reachable adjacent tiles. Confirm the worker visibly walks to each, uses the appropriate tool, clears them, increments Done today once per cleared object, and returns home after work is exhausted. Confirm large logs, stumps, boulders, trees, crops, fences, and player objects remain untouched.
26. Assign `Clear debris` to a Forager and select an outdoor area other than Farm. Confirm the worker clears only small litter in that area, while a Farmer assigned the same job stays on the Farm. Change the Forager's area, then issue a new Clear debris order and confirm the new area is used. Save and reload to confirm the assignment persists.
27. Assign two workers to Clear debris in the same area. Confirm they choose distinct objects while travelling or swinging. Make one object unreachable but leave another reachable; confirm they skip the blocked object and continue without teleporting or repeatedly attacking it. Change one worker to Idle while travelling or clearing, and check that work stops and it returns home. Pause in a menu or event, then resume and check that no debris changed while time was stopped.
28. For Clear debris, choose a chest for a worker, then fill or remove it. Confirm new wood, stone, and fiber drops go to that worker's chest when available or to the shipping bin fallback, while older nearby player drops stay on the ground. In multiplayer, confirm only the host clears objects, updates Done today, and stores the drops; clients see the resulting state.
29. In a test save, open a worker's Roster page and confirm it shows a Farming level badge without covering the worker's name, status, or location. Open Skills and confirm all five skills—Farming, Mining, Fishing, Foraging, and Combat—show level 0 with empty progress bars and no numeric XP counter. Resize the menu and check every row remains readable.
30. Assign a Farmer to harvest exactly one mature crop. Reopen Skills after the harvest and confirm only Farming's progress bar advances by 10 XP toward its next level. Watering a crop, walking to a job, and moving produce to a chest or the shipping bin must not change XP. Check the player's Farming XP before and after; the worker's harvest must not raise it.
31. Harvest one mature regrowing crop, then let the worker revisit it before it regrows. Confirm the first harvest gains 10 XP and the retry gains none. Force a blocked/invalid harvest target and confirm it gains none. Check harvested produce and regrowth behavior remain correct.
32. Hire a second worker and have only one harvest. Confirm their Farming XP differs. End the day, reload, and confirm each worker's five skills persist. Load a roster saved before worker skills were added and confirm all five start at zero XP without duplicate workers or wages.
33. In multiplayer, observe the Skills tab on a farmhand after the host's worker harvests. Confirm the progress appears through the roster mirror, and that farmhands cannot award XP by interacting with workers, crops, or the menu. Resize the game window and check Roster and Skills remain readable.
34. Send one worker to collect exactly one wild forage item, then cut one ordinary tree and one hardwood tree or clump. Confirm that worker gains 7, 12, and 25 Foraging XP respectively, with one award per completed action. Check the player's Foraging XP before and after; it must remain unchanged.
35. Assign Clear debris and clear one weed, twig, and loose stone. Confirm the worker gains 2 Foraging XP and 1 Mining XP, with no XP on failed or cancelled swings. Have another worker clear small litter only to reach a job or the farmhouse; that route clearing must grant no XP. Check the player's Foraging and Mining XP before and after, including after stone drops are created.
36. Have two workers perform different jobs, then reopen each Skills page and save/reload. Confirm Farming, Foraging, and Mining totals stay with the worker who performed the task. Fishing and Combat remain at zero. In multiplayer, confirm farmhands see the host's saved totals without creating their own awards.
37. Assign two workers different chests and leave a third worker unassigned. Confirm each worker's crops, forage, tree, and debris drops go to that worker's chest, while the unassigned worker uses the shipping bin. Save and reload to confirm all three choices persist. Load a save with the former roster-wide chest setting and confirm the old chest is copied to each existing worker. Fill, lock, or remove one assigned chest and confirm every item remains accounted for in the shipping bin fallback.
