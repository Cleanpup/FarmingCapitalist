# Manual in-game checks

Build with `~/.dotnet/dotnet build FarmingCapitalist.csproj -p:EnableModDeploy=false` and copy the generated mod into the game only when you are ready to test.

Fully exit Stardew Valley/SMAPI before copying or deploying a rebuilt mod. Overwriting the DLL while the game has it mapped caused corrupted assembly errors during the 1.2.8 live deployment.

Run the approach regression fixture with `~/.dotnet/dotnet run --project Tests/WorkApproachChecks/WorkApproachChecks.csproj -p:GamePath="/path/to/Stardew Valley"`. It covers passable grass, fallback recovery without initial snapshot membership, and blocked or occupied approach/landing controls. This fixture does not replace the live game checks below.

1. Load a save with at least 500g. Press `B` and hire a worker with the custom appearance flow. Confirm the worker appears in the farmhouse and the hire cost is charged once.
2. Open the worker menu again. Confirm the worker card shows assignment, current state, location, wage, and completed work. Resize the game window and check the menu remains usable.
3. Assign `Water crops`, `Harvest crops`, and `Tend crops` one at a time. From the farmhouse, confirm the worker visibly walks to an existing warp, arrives directly on a safe walkable tile at or beside an eligible crop, and performs the matching work without a farm staging trip. Put a ready crop at Farm (64,20) with its own tile blocked for walking but a neighboring approach open; confirm `Tend crops` reaches and harvests it without repeating a failed route every second. Make one crop completely unreachable while another is reachable; confirm the worker tries another crop without rapid repeated target selection. For a field with exactly one eligible crop, confirm the worker completes the last crop and visibly walks to a farm exit before arriving home.
4. After the worker arrives home, confirm the sprite is stopped in the neutral south-facing standing frame. Confirm the old persisted work assignment remains selected but the worker stays resting and does not immediately leave again.
5. Confirm harvested produce goes to the shipping bin by default. In Storage, select a chest on the farm and then one inside a building; confirm all workers deposit there. Fill or remove the selected chest and confirm remaining produce goes to the shipping bin. Confirm regrowing crops retain their regrowth behavior.
6. Assign `Idle` while the worker is travelling. Confirm the route stops, the worker visibly returns to the farmhouse, and the worker does not continue changing crops.
7. Hire multiple workers and assign them work. Confirm their resting tiles are distinct, clear, and away from every farmhouse door/warp and the tile immediately inside/in front of the exit; the player must be able to enter and leave normally.
8. Make a worker's saved farmhouse tile occupied or blocked, then complete work. Confirm it selects another clear farmhouse tile without overlapping the occupant or overwriting the saved roster tile.
9. End the day and reload the save. Confirm identity, appearance, assignment, shared chest destination, and worker count persist, with one wage per worker per day. On the next day/reset, confirm a persisted work assignment can run again after the previous day's resting latch is cleared.
10. Try hiring with insufficient funds, hiring at the worker cap, dismissing one worker, and dismissing all workers. Confirm failed actions do not charge money and other workers remain intact.
11. In multiplayer, confirm the host alone performs hiring, assignment, dismissal, wages, movement, crop changes, and save writes; clients observe the mirrored worker state and storage choice without changing them.
12. Use `workers help`, `workers status`, and `workers assign <id> <water|harvest|tend|debris|idle>` to inspect and control workers from the SMAPI console.
13. Use the action button on each worker while it is idle, working, and returning home. Confirm a normal portrait dialogue box opens with the worker's display name, the line matches its assignment, and talking again on the same day repeats the same line.
14. Sleep to the next day and talk to the worker again. Confirm its line advances predictably. Test a Content Patcher `EditData` change targeting `Mods/Cleanpup.FarmingCapitalist/WorkerDialogue` and confirm the edited line appears.
15. Hire one Farmer and one Forager. Confirm the upper-right job dropdown in the creation screen saves the chosen profession, and that old saves load existing workers as Farmers.
16. Open Jobs and switch between the Farmer and Forager. Confirm both offer `Clear debris` alongside their existing profession orders; the Forager also sees a forage-area selector.
17. Cycle the Forager's area through Farm, Cindersap Forest, Town, Mountain, Beach, Bus Stop, Backwoods, Railroad, and Secret Woods. Confirm caves, mines, sewers, and interiors never appear, and changing area cancels the old route and returns the worker home until a new order is chosen.
18. Place or find multiple forage items and trees with obstacles around some of them. Confirm the worker chooses a reachable target, approaches an adjacent walkable tile, never crosses fences or water on foot, and never lands inside a blocked tile.
19. Run all three Forager orders. Confirm wild forage is removed and stored; ordinary trees exclude mahogany; hardwood includes mahogany trees, large stumps, and hollow logs; wood, sap, seeds, hardwood, and other vanilla drops go to the selected chest or shipping-bin fallback. Cut several ordinary trees in a row and check that the game stays responsive when each tree falls and its drops are stored.
20. From the farmhouse, send a Forager to Cindersap Forest. Confirm it walks visibly to a reachable existing warp, then arrives directly at a safe approach tile for the Forest job without walking through intermediate maps. After the job, confirm it walks to a reachable existing warp and arrives directly on its reserved farmhouse resting tile. Repeat with that tile occupied and confirm the worker chooses another clear, valid resting tile without overlapping the occupant or freezing the game.
21. On the Farm, block one hardwood approach with weeds, loose stones, or small twigs while leaving another hardwood target reachable. Confirm the worker takes the open route without clearing litter. Then make a target reachable only through one of each small litter type. Confirm it walks to the blocker, clears only what is needed, resumes the job, and stores fiber, stone, or wood drops in the selected chest or shipping bin. Confirm older nearby player drops remain on the ground. For stone or weeds with more than one durability step, confirm logged progress advances through each cooldown and the litter is removed before the calculated safeguard. Recreate a stalled work-route debris approach near Farm (5,45) with another hardwood target available; confirm the worker excludes that failed debris tile, checks another work route, and does not switch to the home route merely because the approach stalled. With no alternate work route, confirm the bounded search eventually returns home without clearing large obstacles.
22. Block every approach to the remaining hardwood with large trees, hollow logs, stumps, or boulders. Confirm the worker leaves those obstacles intact, returns home, and reports the blockage in plain language on the next conversation. Talk again and confirm normal dialogue resumes. Repeat with a save and reload before talking; in multiplayer, speak to the worker as a client and confirm the report is acknowledged once for everyone. Place a chest, fence, or crop across the route and confirm it is never removed or reported as a large natural obstacle.
23. Near 22:00, send a worker toward blocked forager work and confirm it starts the home route when work hours end. Change the assignment while the worker is walking to litter and confirm it stops that job without changing the tile. If small debris blocks the route to all Farm exits, confirm the worker clears only the debris needed and then walks out. If no small-debris route to an authored exit exists, confirm it arrives at a clear reserved farmhouse tile by the last-resort warp. Confirm the warp is never used when a visible exit route or clearable debris route exists, and that a missing-path report appears only if safe farmhouse placement fails.
24. On the hardwood regression save, assign Worker Two to Cut hardwood in Farm. After clumps (4,43) and (8,47), confirm it can select the stump at (14,48) via the grassy approach (13,50) or another reachable approach, without cutting the grass or returning home early. Repeat with every reachable approach covered in grass, including departure from FarmHouse. Temporarily occupy an approach with the farmer or another NPC, then test an actual wall, chest, NPC barrier, or large obstacle: the worker must neither overlap nor clear these blockers. If debris clearing exposes an approach absent from the initial search, confirm fallback logs can report `search-snapshot match=False` with `currently eligible match=True` and then resume the resource job. Recheck Tend crops, drop storage, and the visible return through an existing warp.
25. Assign `Clear debris` to a Farmer. Place one-tile weeds, loose stones, and small fallen branches on the Farm with reachable adjacent tiles. Confirm the worker visibly walks to each, uses the appropriate tool, clears them, increments Done today once per cleared object, and returns home after work is exhausted. Confirm large logs, stumps, boulders, trees, crops, fences, and player objects remain untouched.
26. Assign `Clear debris` to a Forager and select an outdoor area other than Farm. Confirm the worker clears only small litter in that area, while a Farmer assigned the same job stays on the Farm. Change the Forager's area, then issue a new Clear debris order and confirm the new area is used. Save and reload to confirm the assignment persists.
27. Assign two workers to Clear debris in the same area. Confirm they choose distinct objects while travelling or swinging. Make one object unreachable but leave another reachable; confirm they skip the blocked object and continue without teleporting or repeatedly attacking it. Change one worker to Idle while travelling or clearing, and check that work stops and it returns home. Pause in a menu or event, then resume and check that no debris changed while time was stopped.
28. For Clear debris, choose a chest, then fill or remove it. Confirm new wood, stone, and fiber drops go to the chest when available or to the shipping bin fallback, while older nearby player drops stay on the ground. In multiplayer, confirm only the host clears objects, updates Done today, and stores the drops; clients see the resulting state.
29. Hire a Combat Worker from the appearance screen. Confirm Jobs offers only `Slay monsters` and `Idle`, plus Farm, Mines, Skull Cavern, Ginger Island farm, and the outdoor area near the Volcano entrance. Confirm the Volcano Dungeon and independent dungeon exploration are not selectable. Set two combat workers to different areas, save and reload, and confirm each area remains separate.
30. Put a monster on the Farm in a test save. Assign Slay monsters and confirm the worker walks to a safe adjacent tile, attacks only the monster, increments Done today once per kill, gains its own Combat XP, and places that monster's drops in the shared selected chest or shipping bin overflow. Confirm the farmer's Combat XP, monster-kill stats, and quests do not advance from a worker kill. With two workers, confirm only the killer gets that kill's XP and old nearby player drops remain untouched.
31. Set the Combat Worker to Ginger Island farm, then the outdoor area near the Volcano entrance. Confirm it reaches each selected area, fights eligible monsters, does not enter the Volcano Dungeon, and cannot travel there before the boat is repaired. Recall it with Idle during travel and during combat; the normal home route should start immediately and no later attack should land.
32. With the host farmer in the regular mine entrance cave, send the worker to Mines. Confirm it first appears inside that cave, never on Mountain. Descend to a generated floor and confirm it follows to a free tile, fights there, follows subsequent host-floor transitions, and leaves no duplicate on the old floor. Also assign the worker while the host is already on level 1: it must stage in the entrance cave and remain there until the host's next floor warp, rather than jumping straight to level 1. Repeat at the Skull Cavern entrance and a generated Skull Cavern floor. While the host is elsewhere, confirm a dungeon worker waits rather than creating or entering a generated floor alone.
33. Let a monster overlap the worker until its HP reaches zero. Confirm combat stops and the worker immediately teleports to a safe farmhouse tile, stays off duty for the rest of the day, and still stays off duty after a same-day save/reload. On the next day the worker may work again. Confirm this defeat teleport is distinct from the ordinary visible Idle return.
34. Near 22:00, check that the worker starts the normal return in time to reach home by midnight (12:00 AM). If a route stalls, confirm the worker is placed on a clear farmhouse tile at the midnight deadline, without duplicating it or overwriting another worker. In multiplayer, confirm only the host moves, damages monsters, collects drops, awards XP, and saves defeat state; clients observe those changes.
35. In the Mine entrance cave, assign Slay monsters while the host is present. The worker should arrive on visible floor close to the host and wait there without patrolling the entrance border or passing through a wall. Then descend: on a generated floor with no eligible monster, watch the worker for several seconds. It should pick short reachable patrol destinations, walk normally without teleporting or stepping onto a blocked or blank map tile, and try again after reaching each point. Spawn or approach a monster during patrol: the worker should stop that patrol and pursue the monster. Assign Idle or approach 22:00 during patrol and confirm the return route takes priority.
36. With the Combat Worker on Mines level 1, descend to level 2. Confirm it follows the host onto a visible clear tile connected to the ladder area, with at least one clear adjacent tile to move into; if no such tile exists, it should stay off the new floor. Repeat on a floor with monsters and confirm it can move away from its landing. Its profile must remain present, and both Idle and Slay monsters orders must work. Reopen Workers after the old generated level unloads; the same worker ID must remain available with no duplicate shell on either floor. Repeat while saving and loading after a floor transition.
37. In a test save, open a worker's Roster page and confirm it shows a Farming level badge without covering the worker's name, status, or location. Open Skills and confirm all five skills—Farming, Mining, Fishing, Foraging, and Combat—show level 0 with empty progress bars and no numeric XP counter. Resize the menu and check every row remains readable.
38. Assign a Farmer to harvest exactly one mature crop. Reopen Skills after the harvest and confirm only Farming's progress bar advances by 10 XP toward its next level. Watering a crop, walking to a job, and moving produce to a chest or the shipping bin must not change XP. Check the player's Farming XP before and after; the worker's harvest must not raise it.
39. Harvest one mature regrowing crop, then let the worker revisit it before it regrows. Confirm the first harvest gains 10 XP and the retry gains none. Force a blocked/invalid harvest target and confirm it gains none. Check harvested produce and regrowth behavior remain correct.
40. Hire a second worker and have only one harvest. Confirm their Farming XP differs. End the day, reload, and confirm each worker's five skills persist. Load a roster saved before worker skills were added and confirm all five start at zero XP without duplicate workers or wages.
41. In multiplayer, observe the Skills tab on a farmhand after the host's worker harvests. Confirm the progress appears through the roster mirror, and that farmhands cannot award XP by interacting with workers, crops, or the menu. Resize the game window and check Roster and Skills remain readable.
42. Send one worker to collect exactly one wild forage item, then cut one ordinary tree and one hardwood tree or clump. Confirm that worker gains 7, 12, and 25 Foraging XP respectively, with one award per completed action. Check the player's Foraging XP before and after; it must remain unchanged.
43. Assign Clear debris and clear one weed, twig, and loose stone. Confirm the worker gains 2 Foraging XP and 1 Mining XP, with no XP on failed or cancelled swings. Have another worker clear small litter only to reach a job or the farmhouse; that route clearing must grant no XP. Check the player's Foraging and Mining XP before and after, including after stone drops are created.
44. Have two workers perform different jobs, then reopen each Skills page and save/reload. Confirm Farming, Foraging, and Mining totals stay with the worker who performed the task. Fishing remains at zero; Combat remains at zero for workers without the Slay monsters job. In multiplayer, confirm farmhands see the host's saved totals without creating their own awards.
45. On a mine floor with a disguised stone crab, confirm the combat worker ignores it while it is stationary and hidden. Reveal or activate the crab with the farmer; the worker may then pursue and kill it. Confirm ordinary visible monsters remain valid targets throughout.
46. On a mine floor with a moving slime, watch the worker pursue it. If every adjacent approach becomes temporarily blocked, the worker should wait nearby and retry shortly instead of walking away on patrol. It should resume attacking when the slime moves into reach, with no unexplained long pause between swings while adjacent. Then let it pursue a farther monster across visually walkable mine tiles, including tiles beneath decorative Front or Buildings layers; it should follow a valid route without repeated unsafe-step warnings, but never cross walls, blank map edges, or mine exits.
47. Give a Combat Worker Slay monsters and approach a monster while the farmer stays farther away than the worker. Confirm the monster moves toward the worker, can strike it at close range, and the health bar at the worker's feet and in its profile both decrease from a maximum of exactly 100 HP. The farmer's health must not decrease from a strike on the worker. Save and reload that day: the worker keeps its remaining HP; on the next day it starts with full HP. Repeat with two combat workers and confirm each has its own HP. In multiplayer, check that a farmhand sees the same bar and HP while only the host moves monsters and applies damage.
48. While a combat worker pursues one monster, let a different exposed monster land contact damage. The worker should stop its old route and pursue the attacker without getting an extra immediate swing. If two workers are present, only the struck worker should claim the attacker; the other should release it and find another target. A hit from the monster already being pursued should keep that target. A stationary disguised stone crab or underground Duggy should never cause a target switch, and the worker must not strike a Duggy until it is fully exposed. An armored Bug may hurt the worker but must not become its target. Repeat after a monster moves away to check route recovery.
49. On a mine floor with a visible monster behind a wall or blocking object, wait until the combat worker's profile says it is waiting for a clear path, then speak to that worker. The dialogue should say `I see a monster but I can't get to it!` and should leave the ordinary worker management menu available. Clear the route and let the worker resume pursuit; the next conversation should return to normal dialogue. Repeat by killing or removing the unreachable monster, switching to Idle, and leaving the floor; none should leave that line stuck on the worker. In multiplayer, check the farmhand sees the same temporary line after the host's state syncs.
50. Watch a combat worker walk, fight, change facing, and move behind map objects. The world health bar should stay centered just under its feet without covering its head or torso. Open the profile and confirm it shows `HP 100/100` when full and never above 100 after damage, save/reload, a new day, or changing the farmer's max HP. Load a same-day save created by the prior 200-HP build with partial health, and confirm the remaining fraction is migrated to 100 HP (for example 150/200 becomes 75/100), then save and reload again without another change. A zero-HP defeated worker must remain off duty for that day.
51. In version 1.2.7, watch a Combat Worker attack from each of the four directions. Its body and visible sword should wind up, strike, and recover, with damage/sound at the impact pose and one hit per swing. Recall with Idle during windup, change floors, or defeat the worker; no pending hit should land afterward. A monster moving out of reach or burrowing before impact should not take remote damage. Confirm walking resumes, the feet health bar stays unchanged, and Farmer/Forager sprites and work animations behave as before. Pause during a swing and verify it resumes normally; in multiplayer, confirm farmhands see the host's swings without causing damage themselves.
52. Revisit the UndergroundMine3 floor-transition failure with Worker 3. Confirm the worker follows onto safe ground with a free adjacent tile, even if nearby ladder tiles fail and it must appear farther across the map. Look for the trace naming the new floor, landing tile, and distance from the farmer. Check narrow corridors, decorative overlays, rocks, water, occupied tiles, and two workers following: neither should appear inside a blocker, overlap another actor, or remain in an isolated one-tile pocket. Confirm it can walk and fight after landing and no duplicate remains on the previous floor.
53. Let a Combat Worker reach zero HP. The host should receive one HUD notification with exactly the same text as the Info log, e.g. `Worker 3 was defeated and is off duty until tomorrow.` Confirm the worker returns home and stays off duty that day, with no repeated notices on later ticks or same-day reload. Trace logs should also record its defeat location/tile and attacker. Multiplayer still applies defeat and emits this notice only on the host.
54. In version 1.2.8, watch Combat Worker sword attacks facing down, up, right, and left. The body and arm poses should match a normal farmer's sword slash, rather than a hoe/axe chop; the visible blade should follow the same windup, quick slash, and recovery sequence. Pay special attention to up/down arms and mirrored left attacks. Confirm one hit at impact, cancellation on Idle during windup, normal walking afterward, the feet health bar, mine-floor following, and the single defeat HUD notice. Farmer and Forager appearance/work animations must remain unchanged.
55. After closing the game, installing 1.2.9, and relaunching, have a Combat Worker initially face down, then fight an enemy above it. Confirm both its body and sword use the upward slash. Repeat when the enemy is above but overlaps the worker's tile, and with enemies below, left, and right; direction should follow the centers of their feet rather than defaulting down on tile overlap. The swing-start trace records direction (0=up, 1=right, 2=down, 3=left) and both centers once per swing. A target crossing the worker during windup must not redirect the slash halfway through. Confirm the correct downward animation, single impact, Idle cancellation, normal walking afterward, feet health bar, and Farmer/Forager appearance remain intact; in multiplayer the client should display the host's chosen swing direction.
