# Manual in-game checks

Build with `~/.dotnet/dotnet build FarmingCapitalist.csproj -p:EnableModDeploy=false` and copy the generated mod into the game only when you are ready to test.

1. Load a save with at least 500g. Press `B` and hire a worker with the custom appearance flow. Confirm the worker appears in the farmhouse and the hire cost is charged once.
2. Open the worker menu again. Confirm the worker card shows assignment, current state, location, wage, and completed work. Resize the game window and check the menu remains usable.
3. Assign `Water crops`, `Harvest crops`, and `Tend crops` one at a time. Confirm the worker visibly leaves the farmhouse, walks to the farm, and performs the matching crop work. For a field with exactly one eligible crop, confirm the worker completes the last crop and visibly walks into the `FarmHouse`.
4. After the worker arrives home, confirm the sprite is stopped in the neutral south-facing standing frame. Confirm the old persisted work assignment remains selected but the worker stays resting and does not immediately leave again.
5. Confirm harvested produce goes to the shipping bin by default. In Storage, select a chest on the farm and then one inside a building; confirm all workers deposit there. Fill or remove the selected chest and confirm remaining produce goes to the shipping bin. Confirm regrowing crops retain their regrowth behavior.
6. Assign `Idle` while the worker is travelling. Confirm the route stops, the worker visibly returns to the farmhouse, and the worker does not continue changing crops.
7. Hire multiple workers and assign them work. Confirm their resting tiles are distinct, clear, and away from every farmhouse door/warp and the tile immediately inside/in front of the exit; the player must be able to enter and leave normally.
8. Make a worker's saved farmhouse tile occupied or blocked, then complete work. Confirm it selects another reachable farmhouse tile without teleporting or overwriting the saved roster tile.
9. End the day and reload the save. Confirm identity, appearance, assignment, shared chest destination, and worker count persist, with one wage per worker per day. On the next day/reset, confirm a persisted work assignment can run again after the previous day's resting latch is cleared.
10. Try hiring with insufficient funds, hiring at the worker cap, dismissing one worker, and dismissing all workers. Confirm failed actions do not charge money and other workers remain intact.
11. In multiplayer, confirm the host alone performs hiring, assignment, dismissal, wages, movement, crop changes, and save writes; clients observe the mirrored worker state and storage choice without changing them.
12. Use `workers help`, `workers status`, and `workers assign <id> <water|harvest|tend|idle>` to inspect and control workers from the SMAPI console.
13. Use the action button on each worker while it is idle, working, and returning home. Confirm a normal portrait dialogue box opens with the worker's display name, the line matches its assignment, and talking again on the same day repeats the same line.
14. Sleep to the next day and talk to the worker again. Confirm its line advances predictably. Test a Content Patcher `EditData` change targeting `Mods/Cleanpup.FarmingCapitalist/WorkerDialogue` and confirm the edited line appears.
15. Hire one Farmer and one Forager. Confirm the upper-right job dropdown in the creation screen saves the chosen profession, and that old saves load existing workers as Farmers.
16. Open Jobs and switch between the Farmer and Forager. Confirm Farmer orders are unchanged, while the Forager sees `Cut down trees`, `Collect forage`, `Cut hardwood`, and `Idle` plus a forage-area selector.
17. Cycle the Forager's area through Farm, Cindersap Forest, Town, Mountain, Beach, Bus Stop, Backwoods, Railroad, and Secret Woods. Confirm caves, mines, sewers, and interiors never appear, and changing area cancels the old route and returns the worker home until a new order is chosen.
18. Place or find multiple forage items and trees with obstacles around some of them. Confirm the worker chooses a reachable target, approaches an adjacent walkable tile, and never teleports through fences, water, or map boundaries.
19. Run all three Forager orders. Confirm wild forage is removed and stored; ordinary trees exclude mahogany; hardwood includes mahogany trees, large stumps, and hollow logs; wood, sap, seeds, hardwood, and other vanilla drops go to the selected chest or shipping-bin fallback.
20. From the farmhouse, send a Forager to Cindersap Forest. Confirm it exits onto the farm patio, replans locally, and walks to Forest through reachable location exits. On the Standard Farm, the southern Forest entrance has an NPC barrier, so the worker should use Bus Stop and Town instead of the direct southern exit. After the job, confirm the game remains responsive and reports that no return route is available; the Standard Farm's Forest, Bus Stop, and Backwoods arrivals are all separated from the farmhouse by NPC barriers. Do not expect a return trip until a worker ingress policy is implemented. Where an exit has multiple warp lanes, confirm it selects one whose arrival can continue toward the destination.
